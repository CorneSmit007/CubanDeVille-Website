Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This policy explains how the official Cuban DeVille website handles personal information. The site is operated from South Africa and is intended to follow the principles of the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information collected

We may collect the information you actively provide, including:

  • Your email address when you request a World Key, join the mailing list or ask for Vault access.
  • Information included in an email you send for bookings, features or collaborations.
  • Basic technical data normally generated when a website is accessed, such as IP address, browser type, device type, request time and pages requested.

2. Why it is used

Personal information may be used to:

  • Send music updates, private drops, early links or other communications you requested.
  • Respond to booking, feature, media and collaboration enquiries.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website.
  • Comply with legal obligations or protect legitimate rights.

3. Consent and communication

Submitting an email through the Vault form gives permission to send Cuban DeVille updates to that address. You may withdraw that permission at any time by using an unsubscribe option in a message or by contacting us directly.

4. Service providers

The website uses third parties to provide technical services. These may include:

  • Vercel for website hosting, serverless functions and technical logs.
  • Google Fonts for web typography.
  • An email platform, automation service or webhook provider configured to process mailing-list submissions.
  • Music and social platforms you choose to visit, including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, SoundCloud and X.

Those providers process information under their own privacy terms. Personal information is not sold.

5. Cookies and external platforms

The supplied website build does not intentionally install advertising or analytics cookies. External websites may use cookies or similar technology once you follow a link to them. Their practices are controlled by their own policies.

6. Retention and security

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, dispute resolution or security. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether personal information is held about you and request access, correction, deletion, objection to processing or withdrawal of consent. We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request.

8. Children

This site is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted information may contact us to request deletion.

9. Contact

Privacy requests and questions may be sent to bookings@cubandeville.com.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website, mailing tools or legal requirements change. The date at the top will be revised when a material update is published.

This policy is a practical website disclosure and should be reviewed by a qualified South African legal professional before being relied on as final legal advice.
