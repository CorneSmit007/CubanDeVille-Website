Origin file / 06

Creator first.
Builder by instinct.
Worldbuilder by ambition.

Cuban DeVille is a Cape Town artist working across music, visual identity, technology and business as one continuous creative practice.

The name arrived after a complete reset: a sharper identity built around rarity, high value, vintage luxury and the refusal to make anything generic.

The long-term mission is simple: create one-of-one work that moves people, then build the systems that allow it to reach more lives.

2017

First beats.

The beginning: learning production, music theory and how to turn an idea into a world.

2023

Art Saves Hearts.

The debut album under the earlier identity — followed by the decision to step back and rebuild.

2024

The reset.

A physical, financial and creative rebuild. The old identity was retired because it no longer matched the ambition.

2025

Cuban DeVille is born.

A new name, a new visual language and the start of the 21 and CORNELIUS era.

2026

The world opens.

PUT ME IN VOGUE, SPEED RACER, CARBONE, USED TO THIS and BRAZIL expand the current sound.
