Refund Policy

Draft — last edited 2026
This is a starting-point draft using common practice for small digital/creative shops — it is not a reviewed legal document, and the specific timeframes below are placeholders. Decide the real numbers and have this checked before relying on it.

Digital products

Vocal presets and other digital downloads are generally non-refundable once delivered, since they can't be "returned." If a file is faulty or doesn't match its description, contact us and we'll make it right.

Feature commissions

Because a feature commission involves real studio time, deposits are typically non-refundable once work has begun. Full refunds are reasonable before work starts. The exact policy (revisions included, refund window, what happens on missed deadlines) still needs to be decided and published here.

Physical goods

If physical merchandise is added to the shop in future, a standard approach is accepting returns of unused, unworn items within a set window (commonly 7–14 days) at the buyer's cost, unless the item arrived faulty. The exact window isn't set yet.

How to request a refund

Email bookings@cubandeville.com with your order reference and the reason for the request.
