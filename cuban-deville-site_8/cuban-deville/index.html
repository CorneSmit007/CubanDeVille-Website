✦About

Who is Cuban DeVille

Cuban DeVille is a creator from Cape Town, South Africa — working across music, brand, and technology as a single continuous practice rather than separate lanes.

Beyond the music, he's an entrepreneur — holding majority shares in several successful businesses, and the founder of a well-known AI automation agency.

At the center of it is the music: vocal-driven, deliberate, built take by take rather than rushed. Everything else — the vocal chains, the Vault, the world this site lives in — exists to support that same standard.

The mission is simple: create things that outlast the moment they were made in.

✦Story

Timeline & Discography

2017
First beats
Started making beats — the first sign of learning music theory and beginning to shape the dream of who he wanted to become.
2019
First recording — "Lambo"
Recorded on a pair of borrowed earphones in a friend's home studio. Released under the name $WISH — the first song out into the world.
2020
"Merky"
Second release, out during lockdown. The first real traction — friends, family, and a growing local following.
2022
"Dark Horse"
First release distributed across every streaming platform. Watching the numbers climb in real time made it feel real for the first time.
2022
"Life In A Tonka"
A release that became a huge influence on the sound he'd eventually settle into. Made alongside longtime friend and collaborator Syntexx (formerly Prod. Texx), a producer from Pretoria, South Africa — the two met on PlayStation in 2017, playing Fortnite, and have worked on projects and songs together ever since.
2023
"Art Saves Hearts"
Debut album, released as $WISH — an acronym hidden in plain sight. A quiet stretch followed, stepping back from the spotlight.
2024
The reset
A month away from everything. A full rebuild — physically, financially, mentally. The old identity no longer fit, so it was retired for good.
2025
Cuban DeVille is born
A new name, drawn from Cuban links and Cruella de Vil — vintage, high-fashion, high-value. First release under the name: a self-titled project, "21," marking the year he turned twenty-one.
2025
"Cornelius"
A self-titled follow-up project, continuing to define the sound.
2026
"Corporate"
The true Cuban DeVille sound, fully evolved. A new era, starting now.
